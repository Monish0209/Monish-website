Kulasai Dasara Kulasekarapattinam, Tamil Nadu

When Humans Become God

What is devotion? What is belief? Every year in the coastal town of Kulashekarapattinam, Tamil Nadu, thousands of devotees attempt to answer these questions through the grand celebration of Kulasai Dasara. Through elaborate costumes, body paint, and ritual practices, they transform themselves into gods, goddesses, and local deities before making their pilgrimage to the temple.

From the outside, this transformation may appear to be purely physical. Yet beneath the makeup and costumes lies a deeper act of faith. Devotees believe that the deity enters their body and joins them in serving Kulasai Mutharamman in her battle against evil. Through devotion and belief, ordinary people transform into the god they embody, both externally and internally.

This series explores that journey, where faith gradually blurs the boundary between the devotee and the divine.

"The series follows this transformation in stages. It begins with the lighter, more human side of the celebration—devotees still carrying the gestures, humor and everyday behavior of the people beneath the costumes. As the pilgrimage progresses, however, the mood begins to change. The expressions become more intense, the gestures more ritualistic, and the boundary between the person and the deity they embody begins to disappear. By the final frames, the human presence seems almost secondary, leaving behind something that feels increasingly divine, mysterious and ferocious."
