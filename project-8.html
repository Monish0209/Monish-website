Vivo × IFP Collaboration Vivo X300 Pro

Shot on Vivo

India Film Project — Official Campaign Series

My journey as a finalist at the India Film Project opened another unexpected door. Following the 50-Hour Challenge, I was invited to be part of the vivo × IFP Shot on vivo campaign for the newly launched vivo X300 Pro.

As part of the campaign, vivo provided me with the X300 Pro to experiment with as a photographic tool and create a body of work entirely using the smartphone.

For me, the interesting part was not simply photographing with a phone, but approaching it with the same eye I have always brought to my photography. Among its different capabilities, I particularly enjoyed the dedicated Street Photography mode, which suited the way I naturally observe and photograph the world around me.

"The photographs that follow were all created using the vivo X300 Pro, exploring how familiar subjects, moments, and perspectives could be translated through a completely different photographic tool."
