Behind The Lens:
Monish Kumar

I'm a visual storyteller with nearly a decade behind the camera, drawn to the quiet stories that often go unnoticed. My work primarily revolves around travel, street, and documentary photography. I'm fascinated by the simplicity of the everyday — the lives of ordinary people, the places they inhabit, and the little moments that are so easily overlooked. I look for these stories in their raw and rustic form, without trying to make them more beautiful than they already are. Read story →
