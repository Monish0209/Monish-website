West Coast, India Kerala Edition

A Love Story Between Sunset & Beach

Being someone who has spent years watching sunrises along the east coast of Chennai, I often found myself drawn to the quiet moments when the sun emerged from the sea. It was only natural, then, to wonder what its opposite would feel like—to watch the sun disappear into the sea along the western coast of India.

Kerala, with its long coastline and mountains rising along its eastern edge, offered the perfect landscape to explore that thought. What began as an unplanned detour during a three-day ride slowly became a collection of moments gathered over several years.

This series brings together sunsets witnessed across the beaches of Kerala, through different seasons, places, skies, and moods. Each beach offered a different setting, yet the connection remained the same—the brief meeting of the setting sun, the sea, and the sky, transforming the landscape into constantly changing shades of gold, orange, red, and blue.

"Through this photographic series, I have tried to capture that quiet relationship between the sunset and the sea—a moment that lasts only a few minutes, yet leaves behind a different painting every evening."
